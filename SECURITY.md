# Security Policy

## Reporting a Vulnerability

Email **security@repl.it** with a description of the issue, its potential impact, and steps to reproduce. Please do not open a public GitHub issue.

## What to Expect

- Acknowledgment within 7 business days
- Initial assessment within 14 business days
- Remediation is performed based on severity

## Disclosure:

We follow coordinated disclosure: we investigate, ship a fix, then disclose publicly — crediting the reporter unless they prefer anonymity. Please allow reasonable time to remediate before any public disclosure.

*Questions: security@repl.it*
